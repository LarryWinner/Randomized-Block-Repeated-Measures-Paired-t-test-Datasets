Dataset:  antiper1.dat

Source: E. Jungermann (1974), "Antiperspirants: New Trends in Formulation
and Testing Technology", Journal of the Societ of Cosmetic Chemists,
Vol. 25, pp 621-638.

Description: In vivo results of comparison of two formulations of
antiperspirants on 20 subjects during a test period (mean scores for
subjects, 1=weak,...,5=strong and disagreeable odor)

Variables/Columns

Subject ID  7-8
Dry Powder score   14-16
Powder-in-oil score  22-24
