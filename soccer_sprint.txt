Dataset: soccer_sprint.dat

Source: C.B. Wragg, N.S. Maxwell, J.H. Doust (2000). "Evaluation of the
Reliability and Validity of a Soccer-Specific Field Test of Repeated
Sprint Ability," European Journal of Applied Physiology, Vol. 83,
pp. 77-83.

Description: Times for 7 soccer players across six trials for
the Bangsbo Sprint test. Trials were treated as fixed effects
to determine whether speeds were decreasing over trials.

Variables/Columns
Player 8
Trial    16
Time (seconds)   18-24
