Dataset:   drink_smell.csv

Source: A.Cavazzana, M. Larsson, E. Hoffmann, T. Hummel, A.Haehner (2017).
"The Vessel's Shape Influences the Smell and Tase of Cola,
Food Quality and Preference, Vol. 59, pp. 8-13.

Description: 
3-factor repeated measures study relating smell intensity of drinks to drink type and container
in subjects. Factors:
Drink: 1=Cola, 2=Water
Container: 1=Coca-Cola glass, 2=Water glass, 3=Bottle
Subject: 1:100 Raters (each rated each of the Drink/Container combinations)
Response: Intensity Rating (0-100)
Note: Data generated to obtain means, sd's and the Analysis of Variance
     (some observations fall out of 0-100 range)

Variable Names
drink
container
subject
intensity
