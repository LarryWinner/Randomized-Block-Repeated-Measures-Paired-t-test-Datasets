Dataset: cat_water.csv

Source: C. Pachel and J. Neilson (2010). "Comparison of Feline Water 
Consumption Between Still and Flowing Water Sources: A Pilot Study,"
Journal of Veterinary Behavior, Vol. 5, pp. 130-133.

Description: Paired experiment study in 9 cats comparing average
daily water drunk when exposed to still or flowing water over 4 days.
For each cat, 2 days under each condition.

Variables
cat    (id)
still   (mL)
flowing   (mL)
