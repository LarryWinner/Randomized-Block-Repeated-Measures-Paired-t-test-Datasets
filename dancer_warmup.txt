Dataset: dancer_warmup.dat

Source: N. Morrin and E. Redding (2013). "Acute Effects of Warm-Up Stretch
Protocols on Balnce, Vertical Jump Height and Range of Motion in Dancers,"
Journal of Dance Medicine & Science, Vol. 17, #1, pp. 34-40

Description: Comparison of 4 Stretching protocols (1=Static Stretching,
2=Dynamic Stretching, 3=Combination Stretching, 4=No Stretch) among
10 dancers (blocks) on vertical jump height.
Data simulated to match paper results.

Variables/Columns
Stretch Protocol    8
Dancer        15-16
Vertical Jump Height (cm)   18-24
