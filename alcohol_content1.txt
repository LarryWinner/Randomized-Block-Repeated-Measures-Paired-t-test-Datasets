Dataset: alcohol_content1.csv

Source: M.D. Taylor and B.T. Hodgson (1995). "Blood/Breath Correlations: Intoxilyzer 5000C,
Alcotest 7110 and Breathalyzer 900A Breath Alcohol Analyzers," Canadian Society of 
Forensic Science Journal, Vol. 28, #2, pp. 153-164.

Description: Alcohol concentrations (mg%) taken from 15 subjects, twice each by 4 methods.
Methods (Instruments): Blood (1), 900A (2), 5000C (3), 7110 (4).

Variable Names:
subjectID
instrument   (see labels above)
replicate   (not a factor in study)
alcCont   (mg%)
