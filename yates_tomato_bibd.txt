Dataset: yates_tomato_bibd.csv

Source: F. Yates (1940). "The Recovery of Inter-Block Information in
Balanced Incomplete Block Designs," Annals of Eugenics, Vol. 10, #1, 
pp. 317-325.

Description: Results of a BIBD with 21 tomato varieties in 21 blocks of size 5.
Each variety is observed 5 times, and each pair of varieties appear together
in 1 block.
