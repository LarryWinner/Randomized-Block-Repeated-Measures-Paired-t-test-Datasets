Dataset:  carspeed.csv

Source: H. Ding, X. Zhao, J. Rong, J. Ma (2013). "Experimental Research
on the Effectiveness of Speed Reduction Markings Based on Driving
Simulation: A Case Study," Accident Analysis and Prevention,
Vol. 60, pp. 211-218.

Description: Randomized Block Design, with 3 treatments of
speed redumction markings (None, Longitudinal Traverse) in
15 subjects. Response is average acceleration (m/sec^2)

Variables
Trt   (1=None, 2=Longitudinal, 3=Traverse)
Subject
accelerate
