Dataset:  soccer_4v4.dat

Source: C. Hodgson, R. Akenhead, K. Thomas (2014). "Time-Motion Analysis
of Acceleration Demands of 4v4 Small-Sided Soccer Games Played on Different
Pitch Sizes," Human Movement Science, Vol. 33, pp. 25-32.

Description: Total Distance Covered in small-sided soccer games on 3
field sizes (30x20m, 40x30m, 50x40m) by 8 University level soccer players
in 4 minute x 4 period games, separated by a week each.

Variables/Columns
Pitch Size    8
Player       16
Distance Covered (m)  18-24
