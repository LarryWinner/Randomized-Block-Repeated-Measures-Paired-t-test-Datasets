Dataset:  swimcap.csv

Source: M.W. Peeters and A.L. Claessens (2011). "Effects of Different Swim
Caps on the Assessment of Body Volume and Percentage Body Fat by Air
Displacement Plethysmography," Journal of Sports Sciences, Vol. 29, #2,
pp. 192-196.

Description: Results of Randomized Block experiment with 3 swim cap
conditions (1=No cap, 2=Lycra, 3=Silicone) with 30 swimmers (blocks)
with response=Body volume (litres).
Data generated to match the Analysis of Variance.

Variable names:
swimCap
swimmer
bodyVol
