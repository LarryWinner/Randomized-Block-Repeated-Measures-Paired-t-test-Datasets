Dataset:  sawmill1.csv

Source: W. Lin and J. Wang (2012). "An Integrated 3D Log Processing
Optimization System for Hardwood Sawmills in Central Appalachia, USA,"
Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, Vol. 82, pp. 61-74.

Description: Randomized Block Design with 3 "Treatments": Actual Sawmill,
Heuristic, and Dynamic Programming algorithm in 30 blocks (Logs).
Responses were lumber value (dollars) and lumber volume (m^3).

Variables:
lumTrt     (1=Actual, 2=Heuristic, 3=Dynamic)
lumBlk     (1-30)
lumVal     (value)
lumVol     (volume)
