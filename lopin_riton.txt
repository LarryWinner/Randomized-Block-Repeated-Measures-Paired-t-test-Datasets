Dataset: lopin_riton.csv

Source: M. Ibarra, P. Fagiolino, M. Vazquez, S. Ruiz, M. Vega, B. Bellocq, M. Perex, B. Gonzalez, A. Goyret (2012).
"Impact of Food Administration on Lopinavir-Ritonavir Bioequivalence
Studies," European Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Vo. 46,
pp. 516-521.

Description: Results of bioequivalence studies for Lopinaviar and
Ritonavir (one Test and 1 Reference formulation) in 16 healthy
volunteers (8 Males and 8 Females). One male and one female were removed
due to aberrant concentrations for the male in Ritonavir arm.

Variable Names:
gender   (1=Male, 2=Female)
subjID
form     (T=Test, R=Reference)
tmax_lop    (time to max for lopinavir)
Cmax_lop    (Maximum concentration)
AUC24_lop   (Area under the concentration time curve 0-24 hours)
AUCinf_lop  (Area under the concentration time curve 0-infinity)
t12_lop     (Half-life)
Same conventions for Ritonavir, with _rit replacing _lop
