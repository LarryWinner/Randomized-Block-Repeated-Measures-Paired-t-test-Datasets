Dataset: pilot_weatherdisplay.csv

Source: D. O'Hare and N. Stenhouse (2009). "Under the Weather: An Evaluation of Different Modes
of Presenting Meteorological Information for Pilots," Applied Ergonomics, Vol. 40, pp. 688-693.

Description: Experiment to compare 3 cockpit weather displays on students' correct recall
in flight simulations. Response variable: Percent of information correctly recalled.
Treatments (Displays): 
1 = ordinary text (Enlish phrases)
2 = redesigned graphical
3 = old graphical
Subjects (blocks): 23 undergraduate students who received each treatment
                   order of the treatments was counterbalanced among students.
Data generated to reproduce the ANOVA and preserve means/sd's and 0 < y < 100

Variable Names:
trt.y   (treatment id)
blk.y   (subject id)
corrRecall    (% correct recalled information)
