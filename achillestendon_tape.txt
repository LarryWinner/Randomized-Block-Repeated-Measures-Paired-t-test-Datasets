Dataset: achillestendon_tape.csv

Source: T. Zhang, Y. Zhao, R. Adams, P. Malliaras, J. Witchalls, W. Fu, J. Han (2025).
"The Effect of Anti-Pronation Anti-Supination Taping on Neuromuscular Control in
Recreational Athletes with Achilles Tendinopathy: A Randomized Controlled 
Cross-Over Trial," Physical Therapy in Sport, Vol. 71, pp. 92-99.

Description: Crossover design with 30 recreational athletes 
with Achilles Tendinopathy, each receiving 3 taping conditions.
Conditions: No taping (i=1), Anti-pronation taping (i=2), Anti-supination  taping (i=3)
Response Y = Weight Bearing Lunge Test (WBLT)
Data Generated to Match Treatment Means and Analysis of Variance

Variable Names:
tape.trt  (levels given above)
tape.subj
WBLT
