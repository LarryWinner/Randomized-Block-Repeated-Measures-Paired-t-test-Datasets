Dataset: swear_power1.csv

Source: R. Stephens, D.K. Soierer, E. Katehis (2018). "Effect of Swearing on
Strength and Power Performance," Psychology of Sport & Exercise," Vo. 35, pp. 111-117.

Description: Results of experiment with 29 adults employing the Wingate Anaerobic
Power Test (WAnT) on a Monark 894E Peak Bike. Subjects were measured in both a swearing
and no swearing condition prior to the test. Data generated to match means, sds, and
paired t-statistic.

Variables:
ID   (1-29)
condition  (1 = non-swear, 2=swear)
peakPwr    WAnT Peak Power (watts)
